# Propane Tank Card

Realistic, **volume-accurate** propane tank level visualization for Lovelace.

- Realistic SVG cross-sections of horizontal and vertical tanks
- Liquid line placed using true circular-segment math (a horizontal tank at 25% sits at ~30% height, not the quarter line)
- Selectable size & orientation (20 lb up to 1000 gal) + Custom
- Percentage overlay, optional gallons-remaining, low-level warning
- Full visual editor — no YAML needed
